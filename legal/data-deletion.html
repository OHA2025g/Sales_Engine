User data deletion

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This page is the data-deletion instruction URL for the SalesEngine Meta app.

How to request deletion

Email info@agrayianailabs.com from the address used on the workspace. Write “Meta data deletion” in the subject. Include:

  • Your name
  • The Facebook Page or Instagram account involved
  • The workspace or company name

What we delete

  • Stored Page and Instagram tokens for that connection
  • Publish records and permalinks we stored for those posts, where they are not required for a legal hold

Posts already on Facebook or Instagram stay there until you delete them in Meta. We cannot erase a live Facebook or Instagram post from this form; remove those in Meta Business Suite or the Instagram app.

Timing

We confirm the request and complete deletion within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law.
