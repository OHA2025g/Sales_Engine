Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy is for the SalesEngine Meta app operated by AGRAYIAN AI Labs (“we”, “us”). It covers Facebook Page and Instagram publishing that customers connect to the Autonomous Revenue OS.

Who we are

AGRAYIAN AI Labs, India. Contact: info@agrayianailabs.com.

What the app does

A workspace admin connects their own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account. After a human approves a draft, the app can publish that text, link, or image to those accounts. The app does not post to other people’s personal profiles.

Data we receive from Meta

  • Page and Instagram account ids needed to publish
  • Page access tokens the admin grants
  • Post ids and permalinks after a publish
  • Error messages if Meta refuses a post

We do not use this data to sell ads to third parties. We do not sell personal data.

Data we send to Meta

The approved post text, optional link, and optional image URL, plus the token for the connected Page or Instagram account.

Storage and retention

Tokens and publish results are stored in the customer’s tenant workspace so the same Page can be used again. Tokens are removed when the admin disconnects the integration or asks us to delete the workspace data.

Who can see a post

Once Meta accepts a publish, visibility is controlled by Facebook and Instagram, not by this policy. A public Instagram account can show the post to anyone. A Facebook Page post may stay visible only to app admins until the Meta app is published and Live.

Your rights

Email info@agrayianailabs.com to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to delete it. See also Data deletion.
